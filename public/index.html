🧭 Web-only farm control

Use the form to record whether your pump is ON or OFF, the tank level, soil moisture and water flow. The website evaluates those values and updates alerts and recommendations.

This website does not operate a physical pump. “Pump ON/OFF” is a status you enter for monitoring and leak-detection logic.

No ESP32, sensor connection, MQTT broker or wiring is required.

For leak detection, enter the observed flow rate and pump status accurately. The website can flag suspicious combinations, but it cannot physically shut off water.
